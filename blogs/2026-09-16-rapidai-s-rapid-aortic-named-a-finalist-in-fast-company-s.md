Aortic disease is among the most dangerous and underdiagnosed conditions in medicine, often remaining silent until a life-threatening rupture or dissection. Rapid Aortic was designed to support the identification and monitoring of aortic disease. It automatically analyzes every CT scan that includes the aorta, regardless of why it was ordered.
